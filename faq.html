FAQ

Ask us anything!

Answers to the questions we hear most often.

What’s included in a grooming?

All bathing and grooming prices include the following services: Nails clipped or filed, ears cleaned and plucked, pads shaved, feet trimmed, sanitary trims, full blow out and brush, and haircut when appropriate. You may request the following services in addition to your grooming; tooth brushing, anal gland expression, nail painting, medicated baths for any skin condition, deshedding, and flea and tick funerals.

How much will the visit cost?

Prices vary depending on your pup's breed and condition, length of time for styling, approximate weight, and behavior during his or her visit. On most occasions we are able to provide an over-the-phone price range for your selected services and an exact price when you arrive for your appointment.

What products do you use?

We use premium, all-natural, cruelty free shampoos and conditioners formulated for a variety of skin and coat care needs. Our lightweight gentle cleansers nourish your dog’s fur, soothe their skin, and leave their coat looking shiny, healthy, and smelling fresh as a petunia! Specialty products designed to naturally medicate and deliver therapeutic relief for seasonal skin irritations, including soothing oatmeal, flea and tick, and hypoallergenic shampoos are also available.

Do you accept reservations?

Yes, we accept reservations/appointments! Grooming is by appointment ONLY. Call or text today!
